## Keyword Mapping to Content

For each blog post, identify:
1. **Primary keyword:** The main search term
2. **Secondary keywords:** 2-3 related terms
3. **Related questions:** FAQ-style content within the post
4. **Internal links:** Link to pillar page + 2 related posts

## Content Gap Analysis

Existing posts are heavy on:
- Technical tutorials (lots of content, good for long-tail)
- AI philosophy (differentiating but lower volume)
- Project write-ups (portfolio value, moderate SEO)

Gaps to fill:
- **Business-focused content:** "ROI of local AI", "cost comparison cloud vs local"
- **Comparison content:** "Ollama vs vLLM", "RAG vs fine-tuning"
- **Beginner content:** "AI infrastructure explained for non-technical founders"
- **Industry-specific:** "AI for healthcare compliance", "AI for legal document processing"

## Pillar Page Strategy

Each pillar page targets a broad head term and links to 8-15 supporting articles:

```
Pillar: "AI Workflow Automation"
├── "How to automate customer support with AI agents"
├── "Building a document processing pipeline"
├── "AI-powered email automation"
├── "Connecting AI agents to existing APIs"
├── "Monitoring and maintaining AI workflows"
└── "Cost analysis: AI automation vs manual processing"
```

## Internal Linking Rules

| Rule | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Every blog post links to exactly 1 pillar page | Concentrates authority |
| Every blog post links to 2-3 related posts | Distributes authority, improves UX |
| Service pages link to relevant blog posts as evidence | Content supports commercial pages |
| homepage links to 3 most important service pages | Top-level authority distribution |
| About page links to best case studies | Personal authority → proof |
| Footer has links to all pillar pages | Site-wide internal linking |
