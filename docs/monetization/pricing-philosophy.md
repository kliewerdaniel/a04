# Pricing Philosophy

## Core Beliefs

1. **Transparency builds trust.** Show prices. "Call for pricing" is a conversion killer.
2. **Fixed-price, scope-defined.** No hourly billing. Hourly misaligns incentives.
3. **Value-based, not cost-based.** Price is determined by the value delivered to the client, not the hours spent.
4. **Premium positioning.** Low prices signal low quality. Our prices reflect expertise, not effort.

## Pricing by Service

| Service | Price | Value Basis |
|---|---|---|
| AI Systems Audit | $500 | Diagnostic value. Identifies $50k+ opportunities. |
| Technical Sprint | $5k–$10k | Shipping value. Working system in 2 weeks. |
| Full System Build | $10k–$25k | System value. Production-ready infrastructure. |
| Fractional AI Engineer | $5k/mo | Availability value. On-demand expertise. |

## Price Anchoring

Use the "Good-Better-Best" presentation:
- **Good:** Technical Sprint ($5k) — for teams with clear scope
- **Better:** Full System Build ($15k) — for complete solutions
- **Best:** Fractional Engineer ($5k/mo) — for ongoing needs

This anchors the middle option as the "reasonable" choice.

## Discounting Rules

| Rule | Rationale |
|---|---|
| No discounts for first client | Sets a bad precedent |
| 10% discount for 100% upfront | Improves cash flow |
| No discount for scope expansion | Scope expansion = new sprint |
| Discount for nonprofit/education ≤ 15% | Genuine mission alignment |
| No race-to-the-bottom pricing | We're premium, not cheap |

## Price Increases

- Annual price review (January)
- 10-20% increase based on:
  - Increased expertise / demand
  - Inflation
  - Expanded capabilities
- Existing clients locked in at current price for 6 months

## Positioning Against Free

Prospective clients may ask: "Why pay when I can use ChatGPT?"
Response: "ChatGPT answers questions. We build systems that make your organization autonomous in specific domains. They're different products."

## Positioning Against Cheap Alternatives

- Fiverr/Upwork AI freelancers → Limited scope, no architecture, no handoff
- No-code AI tools → Limited customization, vendor lock-in
- DIY → Takes 6-12 months and a dedicated hire

## Refund Policy

- **Digital products:** 30-day, no-questions-asked
- **Consulting:** No refunds after work begins (work is custom)
- **Retainers:** Month-to-month, cancel anytime

## Payment Methods

- Credit/debit cards (Stripe)
- Bank transfer (wire/ACH) for larger engagements
- Invoicing: Net-15 for enterprise clients
