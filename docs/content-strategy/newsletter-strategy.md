# Newsletter Strategy

## Purpose

The newsletter is the primary owned distribution channel. It's a relationship builder, lead nurturer, and product launch platform. Every subscriber is a potential client.

## Newsletter Types

### Weekly Digest
- **Frequency:** Every Tuesday
- **Content:** 
  - 1 featured blog post (link + summary)
  - 2-3 curated links (interesting AI finds)
  - 1 personal note (what I'm building/thinking)
- **Length:** 300-500 words
- **Goal:** Consistent touchpoint, traffic generation
- **CTA:** Read the full post, reply with thoughts

### Monthly Deep Dive
- **Frequency:** First Thursday of month
- **Content:** 
  - Extended analysis on one topic
  - Architecture breakdown or tutorial
  - Exclusive content not on blog
- **Length:** 1000-2000 words
- **Goal:** Demonstrate depth, provide unique value
- **CTA:** Book consultation, buy product

### Product/Launch Emails
- **Frequency:** As needed (1-2 per quarter)
- **Content:** New product, template, or course announcement
- **Length:** 400-700 words
- **Goal:** Direct sales
- **CTA:** Buy now, early bird discount

| 3 | Day 7 | Case study | Credibility |
| 4 | Day 14 | "How I work" + services overview | Soft pitch |
| 5 | Day 30 | Free consultation offer | Direct CTA |

## Segmentation

| Segment | Definition | Content Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Technical builders | Downloaded code/templates | Tutorials, architecture posts |
| Business decision-makers | Downloaded checklists/guides | ROI, case studies, services |
| Philosophical readers | Read essay-type posts | Big ideas, sovereignty |
| Existing clients | Past engagement | Advanced content, upsell |
| Cold subscribers | No opens in 60 days | Re-engagement campaign |

## Technical Setup

- **Provider:** ConvertKit (for creators, good automation) or Buttondown (simpler, cheaper)
- **API:** Resend for transactional emails (delivery confirmations)
- **Forms:** Custom components on site → API → provider
- **Subscriber count target:** 1,000 in first 6 months
