# Service: AI-Powered Websites

## What We Build

Web applications with AI integrated at the architecture level. Not "add a chatbot to an existing site" — sites designed from the ground up to leverage AI capabilities.

## Common Use Cases

- **AI-First SaaS Products:** Web apps where AI is the core differentiator
- **Intelligent Dashboards:** Analytics platforms with natural language querying
- **Content Platforms:** Sites with AI-powered search, recommendations, personalization
- **Customer Portals:** Self-service platforms with AI-guided workflows
- **Interactive Demos:** Showcase your AI product with a compelling web experience
