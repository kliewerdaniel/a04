Your current site has a strong identity and a huge amount of technical depth, but it has a major business problem:

It reads more like an intellectual archive and manifesto than a machine designed to convert visitors into paying clients.  ￼

Right now the site signals:

* “I think deeply about AI systems.”
* “I experiment aggressively.”
* “I understand local-first architectures.”
* “I have unusual ideas.”

But it does not immediately answer:

* “What can this person do for my business?”
* “How much does it cost?”
* “How do I hire him?”
* “What problem gets solved?”
* “What is the fastest path to ROI?”

That is the redesign opportunity.

What the new site should become

The new version should not primarily be:

* a blog,
* a digital philosophy archive,
* or a technical notebook.

Those should support the business.

The actual homepage should function like:

1. a sales funnel,
2. a trust engine,
3. a lead-generation system,
4. and a portfolio of deployed business outcomes.

You need to reposition yourself from:

“Interesting AI thinker”

to:

“Austin-based AI automation engineer who helps businesses reduce labor and build internal tools fast.”

That framing makes money.

⸻

The core problem with the current site

You are targeting too many audiences simultaneously:

* AI researchers
* open-source developers
* philosophers
* local LLM hobbyists
* Reddit intellectuals
* startup founders
* artists
* business owners

The result:

* high intellectual density,
* low conversion clarity.

Most business owners landing there will not understand:

* MCP
* GraphRAG
* persona MoE
* computational sovereignty
* autonomous architectures

They may think:

“This guy is smart.”

But not:

“I should pay this guy $3,000.”

⸻

The new positioning

The site should be rebuilt around this sentence:

“I build AI systems and automation workflows for businesses that want practical results without enterprise pricing.”

Everything should support that.

⸻

Recommended homepage structure

HERO SECTION

Big clear statement.

Example:

AI Automation & Internal Tools for Small Businesses
Built Fast. Local-First. Privacy-Focused.

Subtext:

I help businesses automate repetitive workflows, organize knowledge, deploy AI assistants, and reduce operational overhead using modern AI tooling.

Then:

* “Book Consultation”
* “View Case Studies”
* “See Live Demo”

NOT:

* philosophical essays,
* giant blog feeds,
* abstract identity language.

⸻

SECTION 2 — WHAT YOU ACTUALLY SELL

Right now this is vague.

You need productized services.

Example cards:

AI Knowledge Systems

Internal business search + AI assistants.

Workflow Automation

Reduce repetitive admin work.

AI-Powered Websites

Lead capture + AI integration.

Local AI Deployment

Private self-hosted AI systems.

Content & Research Pipelines

Automated content generation and organization.

⸻

SECTION 3 — CASE STUDIES

This is the money section.

Not:

“I built an autonomous cognitive architecture.”

Instead:

* what problem existed,
* what you built,
* what it solved,
* timeline,
* stack,
* measurable outcome.

Even if the projects are speculative or demo systems, frame them commercially.

⸻

SECTION 4 — SOCIAL PROOF

Right now your experience is buried in prose.

This should become visually obvious:

* 10+ years data annotation/RLHF  ￼
* Full-stack AI systems
* Local LLM expertise
* Austin-based
* Open-source community organizer
* Rapid prototyping ability

This is actually strong positioning.

⸻

SECTION 5 — LEAD CAPTURE

Critical.

You need:

* consultation form,
* calendly integration,
* email capture,
* downloadable PDF/service sheet,
* maybe a free “AI workflow audit.”

The current site is not optimized to capture leads.

⸻

SECTION 6 — BLOG (SECONDARY)

Your blog is actually a huge SEO asset.  ￼

But:

* move it lower,
* categorize it,
* simplify titles,
* separate “business-facing” posts from philosophical/experimental writing.

Right now the density overwhelms commercial visitors.
