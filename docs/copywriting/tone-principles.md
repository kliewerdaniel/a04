# Copywriting Tone & Principles

## The Voice

**Direct. Technical. Personal. Confident without arrogance. Clear without simplicity.**

## Core Rules

### 1. Show, Don't Tell
❌ "I'm an expert in AI systems."
✅ "I've built RAG pipelines that process 50k documents with 200ms latency."

❌ "I provide high-quality consulting."
✅ "Two-week sprint. Working software. Fixed price."

### 2. Specific Over General
❌ "I help businesses with AI."
✅ "I build local-first knowledge systems for organizations that can't send data to the cloud."

❌ "Contact me to learn more."
✅ "Book a free 30-minute consultation. I'll help you scope the problem."

### 3. Active Voice
❌ "Systems are built by me."
✅ "I build systems."

❌ "The architecture was designed."
✅ "I designed the architecture."

### 4. Short Sentences
❌ "Our comprehensive AI infrastructure solutions encompass a wide range of cutting-edge technologies to deliver transformative business outcomes."
✅ "I build AI infrastructure. Local-first. Production-ready. Your data stays yours."

### 5. Technical Precision
❌ "We use smart AI technology."
✅ "The system uses Ollama for local inference, Chroma for vector storage, and LangChain for orchestration."

### 6. No Hype Words
| Avoid | Use Instead |
|---|---|
| Revolutionary | Working |
| Disruptive | Different |
| Game-changing | Effective |
| Cutting-edge | Production-ready |
| State-of-the-art | Well-architected |
| AI-powered | AI-integrated |
| Transformative | Practical |

### 7. Write for Scanning
- Headings that summarize the section
- Bold key phrases
- Bullet points for lists
- One idea per paragraph
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)

## Page-Specific Tone

### Homepage
**Bold, minimal, authoritative.**
- Short statements that build on each other
- Every word earns its place
- CTA is direct: "Book a Free Consultation"

### Services
**Transparent, specific, outcome-oriented.**
- Prices listed upfront
- What's included / what's not included
- "Here's what you get. Here's what it costs."

### Blog Posts (Technical)
**Conversational, detailed, instructive.**
- "Here's what I built and how you can build it too."
- Code is the primary content
- Explain trade-offs, not just solutions

### Blog Posts (Philosophical)
**Reflective, grounded, personal.**
- "Here's a big idea I've been thinking about."
- Connect abstract concepts to concrete examples
- Invite discussion

### About Page
**Personal, honest, mission-driven.**
- "This is what I believe and why."
- "Here's what I've built that proves it."
- Let personality show

### Case Studies
**Narrative, evidence-driven, humble.**
- "Here's what went wrong."
- "Here's what we learned."
- "Here are the numbers."

## The "Don't Say" List

- "Let's hop on a call" (be specific about purpose)
- "I'm passionate about" (show it through work)
- "Synergy" or "leverage" (meaningless business words)
- "Game-changer" (let results speak)
- "We're excited to announce" (just announce it)
- "Please don't hesitate to reach out" (just say "reach out")
- "In today's digital landscape" (say nothing)
