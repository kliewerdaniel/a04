# Monetization Strategy

## Revenue Model Overview

The business operates on three revenue tiers, building from low-friction to high-value:

```
Tier 1: Digital Products ($19–$297)
├── AI Starter Kits
├── Prompt Packs
├── Downloadable Templates
├── Technical Guides / eBooks
├── Boilerplate Code Repos
└—— Low friction, passive, builds email list

Tier 2: Consulting Services ($500–$25,000)
├── AI Systems Audit ($500)
├── Technical Sprint (2 weeks, $5k–$10k)
├── Full System Build ($10k–$25k)
├── Fractional AI Engineer (retainer, $5k/mo)
└—— Primary revenue driver, high-touch

Tier 3: Educational ($297–$1,497)
├── AI Implementation Course ($497)
├── Local LLM Masterclass ($297)
├── Consulting Accelerator ($1,497)
└—— Future phase, requires content investment
```

## Tier 2: Consulting Services

### Service Packages

| Package | Price | Duration | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Systems Audit | $500 | 1 week | Current state assessment, opportunity map, roadmap PDF |
| Technical Sprint | $5k–$10k | 2 weeks | Working prototype or production-ready feature |
| Full System Build | $10k–$25k | 4–8 weeks | Complete AI system, deployed and tested |
| Fractional AI Engineer | $5k/month | Ongoing | 20h/week, architecture, code, strategy |

### Engagement Flow
1. **Inbound lead** → Content consumption or referral
2. **Free consultation** → 30-min discovery call (qualify + scope)
3. **Proposal** → Fixed-price or retainer agreement sent within 48 hours
4. **Sprint kickoff** → Onboarding, access setup, initial sprint planning
5. **Delivery** → Weekly demos, transparent progress, iterative feedback
6. **Handoff** → Documentation, training, deployment, support period

### Pricing Philosophy
- **Transparency:** Base prices listed on services page. No "call for pricing" gate.
- **Value-based, not hourly:** A Technical Sprint costs the same whether it takes 40 hours or 80 hours.
- **Outcome-oriented:** Proposals include specific deliverables and success criteria.
- **No hidden fees:** What we quote is what you pay. Scope changes add sprints.

### Launch Sequence
1. **Waitlist** → Collect interest via newsletter (minimal commitment)
2. **Beta cohort** → Discounted price, intensive feedback, case studies
3. **Public launch** → Full price, self-paced or cohort-based
4. **Evergreen** → Continuous enrollment, automated delivery
